# Agent User Guide — D&M Deal Portal

## What This App Does for You

The D&M Deal Portal is your tool for submitting vehicle deals for approval. Instead of emailing paperwork or calling managers, you enter deal information into the portal, upload your documents, and track the deal's progress in real time. You'll know instantly when a manager reviews your deal, when underwriting picks it up, and if anything needs to be fixed.

## Submitting a New Deal

This is your most common action. Click **"Submit New Deal"** from the dashboard or navigate to **Deals > New Deal**.

### Step 1: Deal Setup
- Choose the **deal type**: Lease, Retail Purchase, Re-Lease, or Lease Buyout
- Select **how many applicants** are on this deal (1, 2, or 3)
- If the applicant has a business, check the business box and enter the legal business name

### Step 2: Applicant Information
- Enter each applicant's **first name** and **last name**
- Enter their **Experian credit score**
- If they have a credit report from another bureau, check "Has Alternate Bureau" and enter the bureau name and score

### Step 3: Vehicle Information
- Enter the vehicle details: **Year, Make, Model, Trim**
- Select the **vehicle condition**: New, Used, or Untitled Demo
- If the vehicle is used, enter the **mileage**
- Enter pricing information:
  - For new vehicles: **MSRP** and **Invoice** price
  - For used vehicles: **JD Power Retail** and **JD Power Wholesale** values
  - For leases: **Net Cap Cost**
  - For retail purchases/buyouts: **Total Amount Financed**
- Enter the **monthly payment** and **term** (in months, e.g., 36, 48, 60)

### Step 4: Trade-In (if applicable)
- If the customer is trading in a vehicle, enter: Year, Make, Model, Monthly Payment, Lienholder, and who drives the trade-in

### Step 5: Open Autos
- If the customer has other financed vehicles, add each one with: Lienholder, Monthly Payment, and who drives it

### Step 6: Credit & Deal Strengths
- Write a summary of the **deal's strengths** — this is your chance to sell the deal to the bank. Tell us what we can't see on the credit file.
- If there is **derogatory credit** (late payments, collections, bankruptcy, etc.), check the box and explain the situation with timelines

### Step 7: Documents
- Upload the required documents for your deal type. The system will show you which documents are **required** and which are **optional**:
  - **Cybercalc** (for leases)
  - **Cyberretail** (for retail purchases)
  - **Credit Application**
  - **Credit Bureau Report**
  - **JD Power Book Outs** (for used vehicles)
  - **Business Credit App** (if business deal)
  - **Proof of Income**
  - **iPacket**
  - And others as needed
- Drag and drop files or click to browse. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX (max 10MB each)

Click **Submit Deal** when everything is filled out. Your deal will appear in your manager's queue for review.

## Tracking Your Deals

Navigate to **Deals** from the sidebar. You'll see a list of all your submitted deals with:

- **Deal Number** (e.g., DM-2026-00042)
- **Customer Name** (primary applicant)
- **Vehicle** (Year Make Model)
- **Status** (color-coded badge)
- **Timer** (how long the deal has been in its current status — green is good, yellow means attention needed, red means overdue)

### Deal Statuses (What They Mean)

| Status | What It Means | What You Do |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Deal just submitted, waiting for assignment | Wait |
| **Pending Manager Review** | Your manager is reviewing the deal | Wait |
| **Kicked Back to Sales** | Your manager or underwriter found issues | Fix the issues and resubmit |
| **Submitted to Underwriting** | Manager approved, underwriting is evaluating | Wait |
| **Kicked Back to Manager** | Underwriter found issues, sent back to manager | Wait (your manager may contact you) |
| **Submitted to Lender** | Underwriter forwarded to a lender for approval | Wait |
| **Approved** | Lender approved the deal | Prepare for delivery |
| **Signed & Delivered** | Deal is complete | Nothing — this is the finish line |
| **Cancelled** | Deal was cancelled | Nothing |

## Responding to a Kickback

When a deal is kicked back to you, it means someone needs you to fix something.

1. You'll see the deal highlighted in your queue with a **"Kicked Back to Sales"** status
2. Open the deal
3. Read the **kickback reason** and **explanation** at the top
4. Fix the issue:
   - Edit fields if information was wrong (click the edit icon next to editable fields)
   - Upload new or replacement documents
   - Add a response explaining what you fixed
5. Click **"Resubmit to Manager"** when ready

## Uploading and Managing Documents

On any active deal:
- Click **"Upload Document"** in the documents section
- Select the document type from the dropdown
- Drag and drop your file or click to browse
- The document will appear with an auto-generated name (e.g., "Smith - 2026 BMW X5 - Credit Application")

To **replace** a document: Upload a new file of the same type. The old version is replaced.

To **delete** a document: Click the delete icon next to the document (only available on your own deals in active status).

## Sending Messages

You can leave notes on any of your deals:
1. Open the deal
2. Scroll to the **Communication** section
3. Type your message
4. Click **Send Note**

Notes are visible to everyone who can see the deal (managers, underwriters, etc.).

## Tips

- **Upload all documents before submitting** — missing documents are the #1 reason for kickbacks
- **Write strong deal notes** in Step 6 — help the underwriter understand why this is a good deal
- **Respond to kickbacks quickly** — there are SLA timers tracking response time
- **Check the timer colors**: Green = on track, Yellow = getting slow, Red = overdue
- Deals that show as **bold/unread** have new activity since you last viewed them
